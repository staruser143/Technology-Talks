No, a chatbot does not need to use agentic AI.
Most traditional chatbots operate on simpler frameworks, while agentic AI represents an advanced paradigm where the system can act autonomously, make plans, and execute multi-step tasks.
## Chatbots vs. Agentic AI

| Feature | Traditional Chatbot | Agentic AI |
|---|---|---|
| Core Function | Simulates conversation based on patterns or rules. | Pursues a specific goal autonomously. |
| Reasoning | Matches inputs to responses (using rules or LLMs). | Breaks complex goals down into sequential plans. |
| Tool Usage | Limited (e.g., pulling data from a specific API). | Autonomous (chooses when to use calculators, search, or code). |
| Independence | Requires human prompts for every turn. | Can run in loops, self-correct, and complete long tasks alone. |

## When a Chatbot Uses Agentic AI
A chatbot becomes agentic when it moves beyond just talking and starts doing.

* Standard Chatbot: You ask, "What is my flight status?" and it retrieves the data.
* Agentic Chatbot: You say, "My flight was canceled, book me the next best option and message my hotel that I'll be late." The agent plans the steps, checks alternatives, books the flight, and sends the email without further prompting.
